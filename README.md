## Summary

These reports cover the footprinting, reconnaissance, network-scanning and password-cracking phases of ethical hacking: gathering public information about a target, enumerating emails, subdomains and DNS/web technologies, discovering live hosts on a network, and recovering weak passwords from protected files — then documenting the findings, the risks they create, and how to reduce them.
